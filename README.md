# Noor (نور) - Modern Islamic Spiritual Companion

Noor is an ultra-modern, beautifully designed Islamic companion application built natively for Android using Jetpack Compose and Material Design 3.

## Features

- **Daily Dashboard**: Real-time prayer times countdown, daily Ayat & Hadith, spiritual streak tracking, and quick access actions.
- **Noble Quran Reader & Audio Player**: Complete 114 Surahs with Arabic typography, transliteration, English translations, bookmarking, and audio recitation.
- **AI Recitation Coach & Guidance**: Interactive AI-powered recitation practice and contextual spiritual reflections.
- **Daily Duas & Azkar**: Curated morning, evening, and situational supplications with counter and audio support.
- **Digital Tasbih**: Multi-dhikr counter with target goals, haptic feedback, and session history.
- **Salat Tracker & Analytics**: Daily prayer log and weekly consistency visualizer.
- **Qibla Compass**: Dynamic directional compass pointing towards the Kaaba in Mecca.
- **AI Spiritual Support & Mood Dua Finder**: Contextual Dua recommendations tailored to feelings and states of mind.

## Tech Stack

- **Framework**: Kotlin & Jetpack Compose
- **Architecture**: MVVM with Coroutines & StateFlow
- **Design System**: Material Design 3 (M3) with dynamic theming
- **Local Persistence**: Room Database
- **AI Service**: Google Gemini API via Firebase AI
